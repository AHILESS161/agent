# Kill / Pivot Criteria: «Регистра»

**Дата:** 28 августа 2026 года  
**Purpose:** protect against sunk-cost continuation

## Criteria

1. **Problem failure:** if ≤3/5 qualified interviews independently confirm the core pre-filing pain, pause B2C build and reassess segment/problem.
2. **Behavior failure:** if 0/5 interviewees paid, actively searched or built a workaround, do not interpret positive opinions as demand.
3. **WTP failure:** if fewer than 3 of 20 qualified non-friend prospects pay 7 900 ₽ after two messaging iterations, pivot price/package/segment.
4. **Quality failure:** if the 20-case blind benchmark reveals ≥2 repeated critical filing errors, stop external automation until the pattern is fixed and re-tested on a holdout set.
5. **Economics failure:** if blended CAC remains >3 500 ₽ or contribution is negative after 50 paid cases, stop paid scaling and change channel/price/COGS.
6. **Operations failure:** if median human intervention remains >90 minutes for qualified simple cases after workflow fixes, narrow eligibility or move to a higher-priced hybrid service.
7. **Compliance failure:** if lawful storage/processing/retention or acceptable registry access cannot be established, do not accept external personal documents or payments.

All thresholds are `[Assumption: precommitted decision rules]`.

## Pivot directions

- Strong pain, weak pure-AI WTP → hybrid lawyer-reviewed package.
- Weak B2C, strong B2B time saving → workflow tool for small IP practices.
- Strong risk-map demand, weak filing conversion → lead/referral product with transparent economics.
- Filing commoditized, response pain strong → response/deadline lifecycle product.

## Review cadence

Review after interviews, first 20 qualified offers, quality benchmark, 20 paid cases and 50 paid cases. Criteria may only be changed before seeing the relevant result or with a written rationale.

## Data Gaps

Threshold calibration requires first-cohort distributions.

## Red Flags

- Changing criteria after failure without new evidence invalidates the protection against sunk cost.

## Yellow Flags

- A single small experiment can produce noise; use the stated iteration/holdout rules.
