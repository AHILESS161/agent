# План действий на 30 дней

**Дата:** 28 августа 2026 года  
**Objective:** turn technical MVP into validated controlled pilot

## Week 1 — Customer and legal reality

1. Recruit 10 matching private-label owners; complete minimum 5 interviews using the prepared script.
2. Produce three DOCX samples: organization, IP, individual; lawyer signs page-by-page checklist.
3. Select and anonymize first 20 historical/simple cases for blind benchmark.
4. Freeze pilot eligibility and visible rejection/escalation rules.
5. Finalize privacy operator, consent, retention and provider data map.

## Week 2 — Quality and offer

1. Run the 20-case blind benchmark; classify every disagreement.
2. Create one landing page with headline «Проверьте риск до оплаты пошлин».
3. Publish full price: 7 900 ₽ service, government fees separately, scenario add-ons.
4. Prepare one anonymized sample evidence report and package preview.
5. Configure funnel events and manual-time logging.
6. Before any real paid document: approve privacy/legal P0, configure encrypted off-host backup, complete restore drill and pass the five-job timeout/retry/restart/idempotency/alert rehearsal.

## Week 3 — Controlled paid concierge

1. Offer 20 qualified non-friend prospects a real paid package.
2. Process first five paid cases with internal lawyer review of every output.
3. Record source, completion, objections, review minutes, rework and refund risk.
4. Send 20 partner messages to designers/seller agencies/accountants.
5. Test self-service vs human-review presentation without changing core price mid-cohort.

## Week 4 — Production decision

1. Repeat PostgreSQL/worker/HTTPS production rehearsal after the first controlled cases and review incidents.
2. Re-check encrypted off-host backup and documented restore evidence.
3. Re-run the five-job concurrency/recovery rehearsal after any infrastructure change.
4. Review interview, payment, quality and unit-economics gates.
5. Decide: controlled production pilot / extend evidence collection / narrow or hybrid pivot / pause.
6. Update assumptions tracker and roadmap from evidence, removing unsupported features.

## Outreach template

> Здравствуйте! Мы тестируем сервис, который помогает владельцу товарного бренда проверить риски и подготовить документы до оплаты пошлин. Ищем предпринимателей, которые уже продают или готовят товар под своим названием. Сначала хочу понять ваш последний реальный опыт — без продажи. Можно задать вопросы в течение 25 минут?

## Day-30 go criteria

`[Assumption]` ≥4/5 interview pain confirmation; ≥2 behavior signals; 0 critical benchmark errors; ≥5/20 paid; privacy/legal P0 accepted; measured human time compatible with pricing; five concurrent jobs complete without loss or duplication and recovery/alerts meet the documented runbook. A result of 3–4/20 paid is inconclusive: extend the cohort, do not treat it as a production go.

## Data Gaps

Access to matching interview recruits; lawful rights/consent for the historical benchmark set; payment-provider readiness; privacy/legal sign-off; measured provider timeout and worker-recovery behavior.

## Red Flags

- Do not replace paid evidence with registrations, page views or completed features.

## Yellow Flags

- A 30-day cohort is directional; avoid scaling before 20 paid cases.
