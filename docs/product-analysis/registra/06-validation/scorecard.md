## Verdict

**Conditional proceed.** The product is more technically mature than the market evidence. Do not stop building entirely, but change the definition of progress: the next milestone is not more feature completeness or server deployment alone. It is five interviews, a blind legal benchmark and the first five then twenty paid cases with measured labor and CAC.

The idea becomes substantially stronger if customers pay for evidence/human fallback and simple cases remain operationally light. It should pivot toward hybrid service or B2B workflow if pure self-service conversion or quality fails.

## What would raise the score above 8

- 4/5 interview confirmation plus behavior signals.
- 20 paid cases with no critical error and positive contribution.
- One channel CAC ≤2 300 ₽.
- Lawyer benchmark and safe privacy/production sign-off.
- Evidence of response attach or paid B2B repeated use.
