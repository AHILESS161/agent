# Business Model: «Регистра»

**Дата:** 28 августа 2026 года  
**Phase:** 4 — Strategy  
**Model Stage:** A — Assumption-Based

## Revenue architecture

| Offer | Customer | Price hypothesis | Unit | Role |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| Risk map | B2C | 0 ₽ | lead | qualification/trust |
| Self-service package | simple B2C | 7 900 ₽ | application | entry revenue |

| Response triage | filed customer | 2 900–4 900 ₽ | event | urgency monetization |

| Expert response | complex case | from 24 900 ₽ | case | attorney-led |

Все цены — `[Assumption]`, кроме публичных competitor anchors из Phase 3.

## B2C unit economics

`[Assumption]` Price 7 900 ₽; variable COGS 500–1 500 ₽; gross profit before CAC 6 400–7 400 ₽; test CAC ceiling 2 000–2 300 ₽. `[Data gap]` Founder labor, refunds, support and legal escalation are unknown; gross margin is not contribution margin.

`[Opinion]` One-off filing alone is fragile. Model becomes stronger if response/status/monitoring revenue increases LTV without hiding future costs.

## B2B economics

`[Estimate based on Assumptions]` At 50 000 ₽/month, software cost per case is 10 000 ₽ at 5 cases, 5 000 ₽ at 10 and 2 500 ₽ at 20. `[Opinion]` Поэтому Pro должен подтверждать ROI минимум на 10–20 делах; меньшим практикам нужен usage tier.

## Scalability

**Scales well:** software intake, document generation, evidence storage, template reuse.  
**Scales poorly:** manual exceptions, legal review, support during deadlines, data reconciliation, bespoke B2B integrations.

`[Assumption]` Routing simple/borderline/complex is the main gross-margin control. Product must measure manual minutes per case.

## Dependencies and partnerships

- Official/current registry data and filing formats.
- Russian-compliant storage and minimized AI data flow.
- Patent attorneys for review and complex cases.
- Seller/branding/accounting partners for distribution.
- Payment, notification and document infrastructure.

## Pricing rules

1. Show government fees separately from service price.
2. Publish scenario-pricing before payment.
3. Never guarantee registration outcome.
4. Sell human review as explicit scope, not hidden insurance.
5. Change B2B price only after measured time saved per matter.

## Data Gaps

Contribution margin, review capacity/cost, response attach rate, partner economics, willingness-to-pay, B2B time saving and churn.

## Red Flags

- `[Risk]` Hidden human labor can destroy apparent 81–94% gross margin.

## Yellow Flags

- `[Assumption]` Lifecycle attach rates and B2B subscription demand are unknown.
