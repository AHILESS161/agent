Probabilities are `[Assumption]`; P0 indicates immediate validation/mitigation.

## Contingencies

- Low B2C WTP + strong paid lawyer preference → pivot to hybrid package, not cheaper pure AI.
- High manual share → narrow eligibility or raise price; do not hide labor.
- State parity → reposition to evidence, human workflow and response lifecycle.
- B2B strong before B2C → run a separate design-partner track, not a blended UI.

## Data Gaps

Incident probability, actual error rate, counsel conclusion, provider SLA, founder capacity and customer acquisition evidence.

## Red Flags

- PII compliance and critical legal error can independently stop launch.

## Yellow Flags

- Most likelihood ratings are judgement, not incident history.
