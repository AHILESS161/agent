Provide co-branded risk map, tracking link, sample report and escalation route. `[Opinion]` This leaves only 420–1 115 ₽ below the 2 000–2 300 ₽ CAC ceiling for overhead, so partner operations must remain light.

## 90-day timeline

### Days 1–14

Analytics events; one beachhead landing; full fee/scenario price; sample evidence report; recruit 5 interviewees and 10 moderated cases; privacy blockers closed.

### Days 15–30

First 5 paid concierge cases; 20 partner outreaches; two lawyer-led sessions; classify every support/review minute.

### Days 31–60

Reach 20 cumulative paid cases; publish 5 long-tail pages; activate 5 partners; launch human-review A/B; start response concierge if an event occurs.

### Days 61–90

Focus on best two channels; reach `[Assumption target]` 30–50 cumulative payments; decide keep/change 7 900 ₽; approve or reject B2B pilot scope.
