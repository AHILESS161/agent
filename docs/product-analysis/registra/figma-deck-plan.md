# Figma Deck Plan

## Shared design system

- Font: Inter (Bold / Semi Bold / Regular), verified inside each file before mutation.
- Palette: Navy `#0B1F2A`, Forest `#0D5C55`, Teal `#18A999`, Blue `#2F80ED`, Warm `#F5F2EA`, White `#FFFFFF`, Graphite `#182027`, Amber `#D69E2E`, Red `#C84A4A`.
- Signature: large cropped ring with a deliberate right-side break and teal evidence node; thin source→conclusion rails.
- Data/Estimate/Assumption: blue / amber / graphite labels.

## Investor deck — 12 slides

1. Cover: dark field, large off-edge broken ring, concise category line.
2. Why now: oversized `25 212` and `+17,8%`; light background with evidence rail.
3. Problem: `35 000 ₽` as the dominant irreversible cost; asymmetrical risk composition.
4. Product: six-stage workflow drawn as a path, not a card grid.
5. Built already: product proof on dark background, technical capabilities around a central `working MVP` statement.
6. Market: nested TAM/SAM/SOM bands with explicit confidence labels.
7. Competition: commodity baseline left, protected wedge right, diagonal transition.
8. Business model: value ladder from free map to filing, response and Agency Pro.
9. GTM: first-100-customer timeline across four stages.
10. Economics: cash-positive vs economic-cost contrast; clearly Stage A.
11. Risks and validation: three gates — customer, quality, privacy.
12. Closing: next proof milestones; no invented fundraising ask.

## Client deck — 8 slides

1. Cover: warm background, simple promise and ring motif.
2. Before fees: visual contrast between 35 000 ₽ government fees and informed decision.
3. What you receive: conflicts with reasons, МКТУ scope, ready documents.
4. How it works: six-stage guided journey with calm progress path.
5. Why trust: every conclusion links to source, confirmed data and human fallback.
6. Pricing: 7 900 ₽ service; fees separate; human review range; no hidden guarantee.
7. When a lawyer joins: simple / borderline / complex routing.
8. Closing CTA: verify whether the case fits; limitations and honest next step.

## Layout variety check

The sequence alternates metric, path, radial proof, nested bands, split comparison, ladder, timeline and sparse closing compositions. Dark slides bookend the investor deck; the client deck stays predominantly warm/light with one dark trust slide.
