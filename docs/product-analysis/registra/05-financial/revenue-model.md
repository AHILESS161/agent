## Future B2B planning guardrails

No external SaaS benchmark is used in the Year-1 model. Until a dated, segment-specific source and a paid B2B cohort exist, gross margin, churn, LTV/CAC and payback targets remain intentionally unset.

## Assumptions requiring validation

Price conversion; attach rates; average response price; monthly ramp; B2B ARPA/accounts; COGS; blended CAC; tax regime; customer eligibility rate.

## Red Flags

- `[Risk]` Year 2–3 revenue is extremely sensitive to unproven channel volume and B2B adoption.

## Yellow Flags

- `[Risk]` Attachment revenue could create distrust if scenario pricing is not shown before initial payment.
