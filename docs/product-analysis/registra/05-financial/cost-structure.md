## Break-even

- `[Estimate]` Monthly fixed cash cost excluding one-time launch and founder salary: about 25 000 ₽; including annualized compliance: about 41 700 ₽.
- `[Estimate]` Cash break-even: 41 700 / 5 389 ≈ **8 B2C cases/month**, ignoring B2B and working-capital timing.
- `[Estimate]` Economic break-even including 400 000 ₽/month founder shadow cost: 441 700 / 5 389 ≈ **82 cases/month**.

`[Warning]` Break-even assumes attachments occur as modeled and lawyer capacity is available at assumed cost.

## Scaling cost triggers

- >30 cases/month: part-time customer operations/legal coordination.
- >60 cases/month: separate support/operations role and object storage/monitoring uplift.
- >100 cases/month: queue/OCR scaling, stronger data-provider SLA, second lawyer capacity.

All thresholds are `[Assumption]`.

## Data Gaps

Actual cloud/LLM/data invoices, acquiring rate, legal review minutes/rate, refund/rework, tax structure, partner payout and staffing salaries.
